# Visual identity and deployment caution

This Lab 3 version is intentionally styled to match the **colour language** of the colleague's LUBS5302M Computer Lab 1 Executive Business Simulation:

- Brick red `#AC3525` — primary actions and labels
- Coral red `#EF4A34` — header gradient / highlights
- Warm brown `#302723` — main text / rich surfaces
- Warm ivory `#FCF8F5` — page canvas
- Light coral `#FCE9E2` — gentle accents and selections
- Dark warm brown `#2B1B17` — sidebar gradient anchor (NOT black)

The palette was verified against the public stylesheet of:
https://danatv.github.io/lubs5302m-executive-simulation/
Reference source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DanatV/lubs5302m-executive-simulation/main/css/styles.css

The header uses a black *University of Leeds* wordmark, embedded as self-contained vector artwork for offline compatibility. The module display includes:

- University of Leeds / Leeds University Business School
- `LUBS5302M Foundations of Machine Learning in Business`
- `Computer Lab 3: The AI Deployment Board`

**Branding permissions:** inclusion here is a teaching design preview, not an assertion that University of Leeds has approved or endorsed the website. Consult the University's visual identity / website regulations.
